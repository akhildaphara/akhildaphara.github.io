## Design Context

### Users
The target audience is recruiters and other developers. The context is professional evaluation and peer review.

### Brand Personality
The brand should feel creative, on-point, innovative, and fresh, and should convey a high level of skill.

### Aesthetic Direction
The visual tone should be modern and memorable, drawing inspiration from cutting-edge developer portfolios. The interface should feel innovative to a first-time visitor.

### Design Principles
1.  **Creative & On-Point**: The design should be visually engaging and immediately communicate a high level of skill and creativity.
2.  **Innovative & Fresh**: Avoid generic templates. The interface should feel new and reflect modern design trends.
3.  **Developer-Focused**: The design should resonate with a technical audience, valuing clarity, and clever details.
